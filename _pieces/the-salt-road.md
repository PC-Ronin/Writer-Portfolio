The caravan left before dawn, as it always had, and Mara walked at the back with the mule because nobody else would.

"You're slow," said the driver, not unkindly.

"I'm careful," she said. "It isn't the same thing."

By noon the flats had turned to white glass, and the only sound was salt cracking under hooves. She counted her steps to keep from counting the days. *Eleven since the well. Four to the coast.*
